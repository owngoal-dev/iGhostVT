iGhostVT 1.6.26 moves to a newer Ghostty, scrolls with your finger on iPad, and asks before dropped text can run as commands.

- On iPad, the terminal scrolled about one and a half times as far as your finger moved. It now moves with your finger.
- With a trackpad or mouse on an iPad or Mac, the terminal scrolled about half as far as a list does. It now scrolls about the same distance.
- Text with line breaks dropped on the terminal ran at once in a program without bracketed paste. iGhostVT now asks first, as it does for a paste.
- In bash, a folder name with control characters could send escape sequences to the terminal, including one that changes the clipboard. They are now left out of the tab title.
- The terminal could freeze when the theme changed, or crash while discarding old scrollback. Both are fixed in the newer Ghostty.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.26-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.26.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
