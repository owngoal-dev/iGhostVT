iGhostVT 1.6.24 tells a program already running in a terminal whether this device is light or dark when a tab opens it.

- A program that follows the system's light and dark, such as Claude Code, kept the colours it chose when it started after a tab reconnected to it — opening the app again, or opening the terminal from another device — until the appearance was switched by hand. Now it is told the current appearance as soon as the tab connects. A Mac or device on 1.6.21 or earlier may still not pass this on.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.24-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.24.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
