iGhostVT 1.4.14 reconnects remote tabs as soon as the network comes back, gives Ghost Remote the Live Activity, and leaves the phone's title capsule to the title; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- After Wi-Fi dropped or the phone moved to another network, a remote tab sat on a frozen screen for half a minute and then retried slowly, often giving up before the network was back. A link is now checked the moment the network changes and given up within seconds if the other device does not answer, and every remote tab tries again as soon as there is a network, with no time lost while there was none.
- Ghost Remote showed no Live Activity. It now puts your tabs on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as iGhostVT does; the extension asks for no entitlement, but the app that signs the .ipa registers one more App ID for it.
- On a phone, the title at the bottom shared its room with the program and device name and was cut to a few letters. It now shows the title alone; the tab overview still shows both.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.14-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.14.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
