iGhostVT 1.4.23 makes remote tabs over a relay hold up on a bad network: they reconnect together, notice a dead link without waiting for a keystroke, and carry an `sz` download through a dropped connection; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- With several remote tabs open, coming back to the app or getting the network back could leave some of them on "Unable to reach the other device": the host turned away all but four connections at a time. The rest now wait their turn and connect.
- When a link died without a word, the tab could sit on a frozen screen for most of a minute, and output seemed to come back only after you typed something. Every remote link is now checked every few seconds when quiet, and a dead one is replaced within about twenty seconds, typing or not.
- Over a slow relay, a long download or a burst of output could get the connection cut by the host while it was still draining. The host now keeps a slow device's connection up as long as data is getting through.
- A file sent with `sz` was cancelled whenever the connection dropped — a phone moving between Wi-Fi and cellular, a few seconds without signal. The download now picks up where it stopped once the tab reconnects, and the file comes out whole.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.23-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.23.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
