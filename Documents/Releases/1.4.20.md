iGhostVT 1.4.20 shows an accessory key's whole text, lets you give a key a nickname and edit it in place, and stops cancelling a finished download when the sender has already gone; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- An accessory key showed only the first four characters of what it typed. It now shows all of it on one line, the button widening to fit.
- A custom accessory key could only show the characters it sends, and changing one meant removing it and adding it again. In Settings ▸ Accessory Keys, tap a character key on the bar to edit what it sends or give it a nickname, which the key shows instead; an escape sequence can read "Up", a long command a short name.
- Over a slow relay, a file sent with `sz` could arrive whole and still be cancelled after half a minute, with a stray Ctrl-X typed into the shell. A transfer whose files all arrived whole now finishes when the sender goes quiet, and nothing is sent to a sender that has left.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.20-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.20.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
