iGhostVT 1.6.40 updates its terminal library and changes nothing you use; it connects to devices on any 1.6 release.

- The terminal library is now libghostty-spm 2.2.2026112401. Its changes apply only to the AppKit terminal view's touch-display scrolling on a Mac, and iGhostVT runs the UIKit view on every platform, so terminals look and behave as they did in 1.6.39.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.40-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.40.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
