iGhostVT 1.6.31 reopens the Mac window where you left it and keeps the terminal at full height under a floating keyboard on iPad.

- On the Mac, the window opened at the same default size and place every time iGhostVT started. It now opens at the size and place it had when you quit. A window opened beside another still opens at the default size and leaves the remembered one alone.
- On iPad, with the keyboard floating, the terminal stopped a keyboard's height short of the bottom of the screen, and after you turned the iPad its last rows sat under the accessory keys. It now reaches down to the accessory keys wherever the keyboard floats.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.31-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.31.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
