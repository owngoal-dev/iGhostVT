iGhostVT 1.4.13 lets dictation type whole sentences, keeps the ⋯ menu's New Tab open while a remote terminal is busy, and switches tabs on a phone by dragging the title; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- Dictating into a terminal typed only the first character or word and dropped the rest. Dictation now types everything you say, and when it corrects a word as it hears more, the line in the shell follows the correction.
- On an iPhone or iPad, New Tab in the ⋯ menu could close again as soon as it opened, most often while a remote tab's program kept changing its title. The menu now stays as it was when you opened it.
- Moving between tabs on a phone took the tab overview. Dragging the title at the bottom left or right now goes to the next or previous tab, with the next title sliding in as you drag.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.13-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.13.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
