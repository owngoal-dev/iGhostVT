iGhostVT 1.6.29 adds Find, and the terminal's text now moves with a trackpad or a finger instead of a row at a time.

- A terminal's output could not be searched. Find (⌘F, or Find… in the tab menu) searches the screen and the scrollback, highlights every match as you type, and steps through them with ⌘G and ⇧⌘G or the find bar's arrows, newest first. Earlier searches are in the menu under the magnifying glass, and on iPhone and iPad, Find… and Find Selection are also in the terminal's edit menu.
- Under a trackpad, a Magic Mouse or a finger, the text jumped a whole row at a time. It now follows them smoothly and comes to rest on a row when the scroll ends.
- On iPhone and iPad, scrolling back with one finger stopped hard at either end, and its glide did not feel like the rest of the system. It now slows down and bounces at the top and bottom like any other list.
- On the Mac, tabs open on several devices were one mixed list. Turn on Group Tabs by Device (Settings ▸ General ▸ Tabs) to keep each device's tabs together and, once a window has tabs from more than one device, list them under each device's name in the sidebar; click a name to fold its tabs.
- A middle click on a tab did nothing. It now closes the tab, and asks first when a program is still running, as the × does.
- On the Mac, the sidebar's edge kept the arrow pointer. It now shows the resize pointer while you hover over it or drag it.
- The Mac window could not be see-through. Set `defaults write wiki.qaq.iGhostVT Window.backgroundOpacity -float 0.85` (from 0.1 to 1) and, for the blur behind it, `Window.blurRadius` (0 to 64, 20 by default), then reopen the app.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.29-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.29.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
