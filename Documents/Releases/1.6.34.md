iGhostVT 1.6.34 centres the Live Activity in a vertical Dynamic Island, and stops comparing dates in a way that can crash on iOS 27.2 betas.

- On iPhone Duo, where the Dynamic Island stands upright, the Live Activity pushed the ghost to one side and the tab count to the other. Both now sit in the middle of the island.
- Built against the iOS 27.2 SDK, a date comparison asked the system for code that some iOS 27.2 betas do not have, and another app crashed this way on an iPadOS 27.2 beta. iGhostVT no longer uses it, in the New Tab menu of Ghost Remote, Shortcuts, file uploads to another device, or the pairing window.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.34-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.34.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
