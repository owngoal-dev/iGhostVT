iGhostVT 1.4.11 keeps its log in a folder of its own, which installing the package now prepares; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- On an iPhone or iPad with custom firmware the log sat loose in the Documents folder every app without a container shares, and where a bootstrap had left that folder owned by root there was no log at all. It now lives in `Documents/wiki.qaq.iGhostVT`, which installing the package creates for mobile, with `Documents` itself where it was missing.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.11-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.11.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
