iGhostVT 1.5.2 makes the new-tab menu's recent directories readable at a glance; it connects to devices on any 1.5 release.

- A recent directory in the new-tab menu was listed by its whole path, so on an iPad a long one like `~/Documents/GitHub/owngoal-dev/iGhostVT` wrapped and was cut off at the folder that set it apart. Each row now shows the folder's name, with the full path underneath, on this device and in a paired device's submenu alike. On iOS 15 the rows still show the full path.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.5.2-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.5.2.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
