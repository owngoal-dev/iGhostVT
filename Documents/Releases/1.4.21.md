iGhostVT 1.4.21 cuts a long tab title short at its end instead of in the middle; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- A tab title too long for its place lost its middle, so a title such as one Claude Code sets read as its first and last few words with the sense cut out between them. It now keeps its start and ends in an ellipsis, in the title capsule, the tab strip, the sidebar and the tab switcher.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.21-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.21.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
