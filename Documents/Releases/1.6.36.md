iGhostVT 1.6.36 scrolls a row at a time again by default, moves smooth scrolling to a switch in Settings, and makes it smoother when turned on.

- Since 1.6.29 the terminal's text moved pixel by pixel under a trackpad, a mouse or a finger, and kept moving after you stopped until it lined up on a row. It now moves a row at a time, as it did before 1.6.29. To scroll pixel by pixel, turn on Smooth Scrolling in Settings ▸ Advanced, under Experimental Features.
- With Smooth Scrolling on, when you lifted your finger slowly on iPhone or iPad, the text could slide back a little before it lined up on a row, and while it moved it could pause and jump, or briefly flicker backward. It now carries on the way you were scrolling until it lines up, moves evenly, and follows your finger more closely.
- With Smooth Scrolling on, when you stopped scrolling with a trackpad or mouse on a Mac or an iPad, the text jumped as it began to line up on a row. It now eases gently into place.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.36-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.36.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
