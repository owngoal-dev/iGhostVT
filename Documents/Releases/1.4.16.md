iGhostVT 1.4.16 hides the sidebar cleanly on a landscape iPhone; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- On an iPhone held sideways, hiding the sidebar left a strip of it, close buttons and all, beside the notch for most of a second before it went. The sidebar now slides fully off the screen's edge, and comes back in from there.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.16-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.16.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
