iGhostVT 1.4.19 brings every tab back at launch without asking, keeps remote tabs connected for a moment after you leave the app, and lets an accessory key send text of any length; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- The launch asked whether to restore the last session, and the answer was always Restore. It no longer asks: every terminal the last run left comes back as a tab, and iGhostVT now reopens the tabs it had open on your other devices too, as Ghost Remote does.
- Switching to another app for a moment dropped every remote tab's link, so coming back meant a reconnect and a replay, and a transfer in progress was cut off. With a remote tab open, the links now stay up for the short while iOS allows an app in the background.
- A custom accessory key could hold at most three characters. It now takes any text and sends all of it; the key shows the first four characters on one line, where a long label used to wrap over three lines of the button.
- On iPad, bringing up the keyboard pushed the sidebar up with it, settings button and all. The sidebar now keeps its full height.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.19-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.19.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
