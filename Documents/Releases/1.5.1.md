iGhostVT 1.5.1 is about a third smaller to download, and closes a tab that could not connect without asking a question that did not apply; it connects to devices on any 1.5 release.

- The roothide package was 8.7 MB, more than half of it six large copies of the app icon. The icon now ships as three compressed images, and the package is 6.0 MB. The icon looks the same on the Home Screen in Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted; it no longer moves with the light the way the layered glass icon did.
- Closing a tab that showed "Terminal Unavailable" for another device asked whether to detach or terminate the session, although that tab was attached to nothing. Close Tab on that card now just closes the tab, and the terminal on the other device keeps running.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.5.1-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.5.1.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
