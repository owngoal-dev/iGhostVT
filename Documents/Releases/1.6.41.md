iGhostVT 1.6.41 includes the MCP server helper in Mac self-update signature checks and in the Mac install and update scripts; it connects to devices on any 1.6 release.

- Mac self-updates checked the Developer ID team signature of `ighostvtd`, `ighostvtd-io`, `ighostvtd-remote` and `ighostvt-cli`, but omitted `ighostvt-mcp`. The updater now verifies `ighostvt-mcp` on its own alongside the other bundled helpers before installing an update.
- `Scripts/mac-update-from-github.sh` now re-signs and stops `ighostvt-mcp` when replacing an installed Mac bundle, and `Scripts/mac-install.sh` stops `ighostvt-mcp` alongside the other helpers during an in-place Mac upgrade.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.41-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.41.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
