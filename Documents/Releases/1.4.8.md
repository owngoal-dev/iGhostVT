iGhostVT 1.4.8 makes held arrow and function keys repeat on the Mac and tidies Settings on iPhone and iPad; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- On the Mac, holding an arrow key, Home, End or a function key in a terminal moved or typed once, so stepping through shell history or a long line in an editor took a press per step. These keys now repeat while held, at the rate set in System Settings ▸ Keyboard, as letters already did.
- On iPhone and iPad, the accent color swatches in Settings were small and crowded into the end of a row. They are now larger, scroll sideways, and each takes a full-size tap; the light and dark theme rows show the theme's name under the title instead of cutting it off at the edge.
- Settings closed with a checkmark that read as saving something. It now closes with the system's close button, rows that appear when a switch turns on slide in instead of popping, and Licenses names each license in full.
- The buttons on the bottom bar and in the tab switcher showed no feedback when pressed. They now dim under your finger, as system buttons do.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.8-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.8.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
