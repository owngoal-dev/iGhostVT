iGhostVT 1.6.27 replaces the Mac's new-tab menu with a popover you can search.

- On a Mac with many paired devices, the new-tab menu grew taller than the window, and each device's terminals sat behind a submenu. New Tab now opens a popover: choose All Devices, This Mac, or a paired device with its count of open terminals on the left, and open a directory or a terminal on the right. With two paired devices or fewer, the popover is a single list.
- The new-tab menu could not be searched. Type in the popover to filter directories and terminals on every device, move with the arrow keys, and press Return to open.
- Two recent directories with the same folder name looked the same in the menu. On the Mac, each now shows its full path beside its name.
- With the Mac's sidebar hidden, New Tab was only inside the ⋯ menu. A + button now sits in the top bar beside the sidebar button.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.27-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.27.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`.
