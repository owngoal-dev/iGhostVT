iGhostVT 1.4.25 fixes a crash on iPadOS 26.0 when certain hardware keyboard keys were pressed, stops the screen jumping when the last tab closes under the keyboard, and brings the terminal back to where you were after a selection scrolled it; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- On an iPad running iPadOS 26.0 with a hardware keyboard, pressing a key the terminal itself does not use — Caps Lock to switch the input language, a modifier on its own, a language key, or a key while a card such as "Terminal Unavailable" was up — closed the app at once. Those keys now go straight to the system, so Caps Lock still switches the input language and nothing crashes.
- When the last tab closed while the onscreen keyboard was up — typing `exit` in your only terminal, say — the screen jumped: the keys went down, the row of accessory keys was left at the bottom on its own for a moment, and the empty page shifted up and down. The keyboard now goes down in one smooth motion as the tab fades.
- Dragging a selection past the top of the screen scrolled back through history, and after you copied or tapped the selection away the terminal stayed there, with the prompt you were working at out of sight. It now returns to where it was when the selection began — the bottom, if you were at the bottom — unless you scrolled it yourself in the meantime.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.25-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.25.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
