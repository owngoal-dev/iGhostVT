iGhostVT 1.6.32 lets Control-Space switch the keyboard language from a hardware keyboard, and stops the first letters you type in Chinese or another input method from reaching the shell as plain text.

- On iPhone and iPad with a hardware keyboard, Control-Space did not switch the keyboard language while a terminal was active; the program in the terminal received it instead. With more than one keyboard language added, it now switches the language, as it does in other apps, and on iPad the language list appears at the cursor. To keep Control-Space for the program, such as Emacs's set-mark, turn off Switch Keyboard Language in Settings ▸ Keyboard ▸ Shortcuts.
- With a hardware keyboard and an input method such as Pinyin, the first letters you typed after opening iGhostVT reached the shell as plain letters, and one also went into the composition: typing ni and Space sent nii instead of 你. They now go only into the composition.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.32-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.32.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
