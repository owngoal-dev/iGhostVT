iGhostVT 1.4.17 keeps a download through the relay running to the end, asks at launch whether to bring back the last session, and writes far less to its log; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- An `sz` to a device through the relay stopped after a few seconds or a minute and the tab reconnected. The host no longer takes a slow link for a dead one, and the app keeps the link alive while it only receives.
- The progress bar of a download over a slow link stood still until the transfer was nearly done. It now follows the bytes as they arrive.
- After a transfer, or a link that dropped in the middle of one, reconnecting covered the screen with binary and changed the tab's title. Transfers are left out of what comes back on reconnect, and one cut off by a dropped link is cancelled and shown as such.
- Quitting closed every shell sitting at its prompt, and a force-quit brought everything back without asking. Quitting now keeps every session, and the next launch asks whether to restore them as tabs or discard them; Ghost Remote asks the same about the terminals it had open on your other devices.
- With Detailed Terminal Log off, the log still filled with tens of thousands of lines during a busy session. It now records the detail only while the switch is on.
- Several settings carried notes that said what the setting already did. They are gone, and the note under Ghostty Configuration says only that new tabs load the change.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.17-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.17.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
