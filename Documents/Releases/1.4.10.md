iGhostVT 1.4.10 keeps the accessory keys up while you type on the onscreen keyboard of an iPad that has a keyboard case attached, and gets its own log back on roothide; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- On an iPad with a keyboard case folded back and Hide with Hardware Keyboard on, the accessory keys could vanish from above the onscreen keyboard partway through typing, or flicker in and out while the terminal resized with them. They now stay up for as long as the onscreen keys are, and go away only while you type on the hardware keyboard.
- On a roothide bootstrap whose home Documents folder belongs to root, Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Logs said the log could not be read, because iGhostVT could not write one. The log is now kept under the home's Library instead.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.10-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.10.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
