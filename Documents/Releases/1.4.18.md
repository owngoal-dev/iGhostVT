iGhostVT 1.4.18 tidies two things in 1.4.17's look; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- The question at launch about restoring the last session put Restore, its default, on the left, where every other alert puts the button that cancels. Restore is now on the right.
- A tab running on another device had a faint band under its title that set it apart from nothing and made the title look smudged. It is gone; the line under the title still names the device.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.18-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.18.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
