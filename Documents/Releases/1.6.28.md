iGhostVT 1.6.28 moves to a newer Ghostty and lets Control-Shift shortcuts reach the terminal on the Mac.

- On the Mac, Control-Shift-A, B, E, F, N, P and V never reached the program, and Control-Shift-' typed a plain `"`: macOS took them as its own text-editing shortcuts. They now reach the program, as Control-Shift with any other letter and Control-Delete do.
- With a Chinese or Japanese input method on iPhone or iPad, the candidate bar sat one row below the line being typed. It now sits on that line.
- On a 120 Hz iPhone or iPad, a flick on the terminal glided at 60 Hz. It now glides at the display's rate.
- On a Mac with a touch display, a finger could not scroll the terminal. A finger now scrolls it, and a pinch changes the text size.
- Resizing a terminal could leave its scrollback out of place, and on the Mac a triple-click across blank lines lost the selection. Both are fixed in the newer Ghostty, which also draws large output faster.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.28-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.28.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
