iGhostVT 1.6.33 stops a tap on a sidebar tab from closing the tab, and moves to a newer Ghostty, so a resize no longer leaves copies of the prompt behind.

- On iPad, and on iPhone with the sidebar showing (iPhone Duo unfolded, or a large iPhone held sideways), tapping a tab in the sidebar to switch to it closed it instead: a shell at its prompt closed at once and ended its session, while a running program or a terminal on another device asked whether to close. It now only switches tabs; a middle click with a mouse or trackpad still closes the tab.
- When you resized a window, or turned or unfolded your device, a shell prompt could leave extra copies of itself behind, most visibly one that spans the whole line, such as powerlevel10k. With the newer Ghostty, iGhostVT now draws the prompt once.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.33-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.33.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
