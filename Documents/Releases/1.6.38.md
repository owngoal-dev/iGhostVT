iGhostVT 1.6.38 simplifies the MCP settings: the switch is now called Local MCP Server, and Forget Authorized Apps appears only when there is an app to forget.

- Forget Authorized Apps showed even when you had allowed no app, and tapping it seemed to do nothing. It now appears only after you allow an app, and goes away as soon as you tap it.
- The MCP section called the switch MCP Server, showed the full server address, and named a specific AI app in its note. The switch is now called Local MCP Server, a Copy Address button replaces the address, and the note is shorter and names no app.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.6.38-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine, and updates itself — or `GhostRemote-1.6.38.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
