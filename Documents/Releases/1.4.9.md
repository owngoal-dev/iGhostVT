iGhostVT 1.4.9 sends hardware keyboard typing to the terminal without a tap first and adds a light or dark appearance setting; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- On iPhone and iPad with a hardware keyboard, a new tab, or one you switched to, ignored typing until you tapped it, and the key you pressed was lost. The first key now focuses the terminal and is typed, and the onscreen keyboard stays down. A locked tab, an open sheet and the tab switcher still keep keys away.
- iGhostVT always followed the system's light or dark setting. Settings ▸ Appearance now has an Appearance row (Match System, Light or Dark), and the choice applies to every window, sheet and terminal at once, and on the Mac to the menus too.
- In Settings ▸ About ▸ Licenses, the list's section header lines up with its rows, and the bash-preexec and iTerm2 color scheme entries link to their own projects instead of the package that bundles them.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.9-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.9.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
