iGhostVT 1.0.0 hands the whole app to VoiceOver: the pass 0.9.8 shipped without notes, written down here, and a build gate that keeps it that way.

- The tab switcher could be read but not used: its cards change tabs on a tap VoiceOver cannot make, so the grid offered no way to switch. Every card is a button now, and activating it opens that tab.
- Tabs were read out in pieces, the name and the program under it as two separate stops. A tab is one stop now — in the bottom bar, the iPad strip, the sidebar and the switcher — and the one you are on says that it is selected.
- VoiceOver could not land on the terminal at all. It can now: the terminal is an element of its own, it is announced as something that keeps changing, and it says when the keyboard lock is on.
- Controls drawn as a bare icon had no name. The log viewer's options menu and the custom key field in Keyboard settings say what they are, and the tab button says how many tabs are open instead of leaving a loose number beside it.
- Settings rows read as a name and a value: the version, the light and dark themes, both text sizes, the Mac helper's status, and every license.
- Decoration is no longer read aloud — spinners, the dots between log fields, the line numbers beside `ghostty.conf`, the icon on an alert card — and an alert card now announces itself as one, with its title as the heading.
- A label that UIKit would never read aloud fails the build, so a control cannot quietly go silent again.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `xros-arm64e` for a jailbroken Apple Vision Pro, or `iGhostVT-1.0.0-macos.zip` for a Mac — the Mac app is ad-hoc signed, so clear its quarantine once with `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/iGhostVT.app`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
