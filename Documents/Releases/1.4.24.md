iGhostVT 1.4.24 gets an `sz` download over a relay going again in seconds after a hiccup instead of half a minute, and smooths the transfer progress bar; it connects to devices on any 1.4 release.

- An `sz` download over a relay could sit at a few kilobytes for thirty or forty seconds and then suddenly take off: after a damaged block or a reconnect, everything the network had already buffered — the whole file, often — had to arrive and be thrown away before the resent part could. The host now keeps only about a megabyte ahead of what your device has received, so a download recovers in seconds. Update both the app and the host to get this; either one alone behaves as before.
- The transfer progress bar jumped from figure to figure, and stood still between bursts on a slow link. It now moves smoothly.
- In Settings ▸ Accessory Keys, a symbol key without a nickname showed an empty circle in the list. It now shows the key's first character.

Choose `iphoneos-arm64e` for roothide, `iphoneos-arm64` for a rootless bootstrap, `iGhostVT-1.4.24-macos-notarized.zip` for a Mac — it is signed and notarized, so it opens without clearing quarantine — or `GhostRemote-1.4.24.ipa` for an iPhone or iPad without custom firmware, which your sideloading app signs as it installs it. To run the relay, use the attached `compose.yml`; the image is `ghcr.io/owngoal-dev/ighostvt-relay`, and the digest is in `relay-image.txt`. SHA-256 sums are in `SHA256SUMS`, and the Mac app's in `SHA256SUMS.macos`.
